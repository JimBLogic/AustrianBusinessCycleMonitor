Austrian Monetary Properties

Scarcity: Bitcoin's 21 million supply cap implements absolute scarcity, preventing the monetary dilution that Austrian economists identify as the root of business cycles.

Difficulty Adjustment: The network's self-regulating difficulty mechanism maintains consistent block timing without human intervention, embodying spontaneous order.

Fee Market: Transaction fees represent voluntary payment for settlement services, unlike tax-funded central banking systems.
